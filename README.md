## 🧭 What Is Cognitive Science?

Cognitive science is the interdisciplinary study of how minds work, generally with insights from psychology, computer science, linguistics, neuroscience, philosophy, and artificial intelligence.

It asks questions like:

* How do brains represent and process information?
* What is consciousness, and can it be explained computationally?
* How do perception, reasoning, memory, and decision making interact? Do we adopt an isolated or holistic view of these aspects?

In **DECOG**, cognitive science is a *meeting point* between structured concepts & analysis and philosophical inquiry.
